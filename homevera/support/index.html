Support

Contact HomeVera

HomeVera is the home maintenance app from 1APP Technologies Inc., Ontario, Canada. It is for iPhone, iPad and Android.

Email either address about the app, a problem, feedback, or a feature request. Both addresses reach 1APP Technologies Inc.

support@use1app.com
adecarlo@use1app.com

There is no HomeVera account. Your equipment, photos, documents, and maintenance stay on your phone, so support cannot open them. Describe what you see on the screen. Do not send copies of private documents unless you mean to.

To remove records, delete them in the app, erase HomeVera's records on that device, or delete the app. To ask us to delete a support email we hold, write to support@use1app.com and say so. The privacy policy explains retention and deletion.

HomeVera is free to start, with an optional Premium subscription. Premium is $4.99 CAD a month or $39.99 CAD a year, with a 14-day free trial. Prices are in Canadian dollars. Taxes may apply where required. Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. Premium is not available to buy on this website. When it is listed, Apple or Google will bill the subscription. The terms of use describe renewal and cancellation.
