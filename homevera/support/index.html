Support

Contact Domoro

Domoro is the home maintenance app from 1APP Technologies Inc., Ontario, Canada. It is for iPhone, iPad and Android.

Email either address about the app, a problem, feedback, or a feature request. Both addresses reach 1APP Technologies Inc.

support@use1app.com
adecarlo@use1app.com

You sign in with an emailed magic link (no password), Sign in with Apple, or Google sign-in. Your home data is stored in Canada. Describe what you see on the screen. Do not send copies of private documents unless you mean to.

You can delete your account in the app. If you have uninstalled the app, email support@use1app.com from the deletion page. The privacy policy explains deletion.

Domoro is free to start, with an optional Premium subscription. Free includes one home, up to 10 equipment items and documents combined, maintenance reminders, manual entry, and the Expiring soon list. Premium is $4.99 CAD a month or $39.99 CAD a year, with a 14-day free trial. Premium adds unlimited equipment items and documents, photo scan, warranty reminder notifications 60, 30 and 7 days before the end date, Book service, and Add to calendar. Prices are in Canadian dollars. Taxes may apply where required. Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. Premium is not available to buy on this website. When it is listed, Apple or Google will bill the subscription. The terms of use describe renewal and cancellation.
