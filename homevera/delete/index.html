Delete your Domoro account

This page is for the Domoro cloud version. It is published by 1APP Technologies Inc., Ontario, Canada.

In the app

  1. Open Domoro.
  2. Sign in with an emailed magic link, Sign in with Apple, or Google sign-in.
  3. Delete your account in the app.

Home data, files, and backups are deleted within 30 days, including from backups.

If you have uninstalled the app

Email support@use1app.com to request deletion. This page does not keep a form. The link opens your own mail app.

You can also write to adecarlo@use1app.com.

Support emails are kept for 90 days.
