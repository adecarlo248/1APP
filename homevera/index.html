What you can keep in HomeVera

HomeVera starts empty. What you add is what you see. These are the records the app keeps on the phone.

Equipment and appliances

Save the model, serial number, location, purchase date, install date, and warranty dates for each item. HomeVera shows which recorded warranties are active and which have expired, based on the dates you enter. Those dates are not checked with a manufacturer.

Documents

Keep a receipt, warranty, manual, invoice, quote, insurance, permit, or service record, and link it to the equipment it belongs to. You can attach a PDF, image, or text file up to 10 MB. HomeVera does not read the words inside a PDF.

Maintenance tasks

Add a task with a due date. Reschedule it, and mark it complete with notes, the service provider, and the cost. The history stays with the home on that phone.

Warranty dates

The home screen includes an Expiring soon list for warranties ending within 60 days. If you allow notifications, HomeVera schedules an alert 60, 30, and 7 days before the end date you entered. The alerts stay on the phone.

How it works

You can type any detail yourself. A photo is optional.

  1. Set up your home Add your name and details such as the address, year built, size, heating, and cooling. There is no account to create.
  2. Add equipment Take a photo or choose a photo of a label, or type the manufacturer, model, and serial number yourself.
  3. Review, then save Text is read on the phone. Each field is marked “Filled in from your photo” or “Not found in the photo”. You can edit every field. Nothing is saved until you tap Confirm & save.
  4. Add the rest when you want Add maintenance, documents, and a service provider. The provider is typed in. HomeVera does not import contacts.

Labels

Take a photo or choose a photo

Photograph an equipment label, or choose one JPEG, PNG, or WebP photo up to 5 MB. HomeVera reads the printed text on the phone. English labels are the ones it is built to read. The photo is not uploaded.

The review screen is editable. Fields it could read are marked “Filled in from your photo”. Fields it could not read are marked “Not found in the photo”. Leave anything unknown blank. Nothing is saved until you tap Confirm & save. You can cancel instead.

The label photo is not kept after you confirm or cancel, unless you separately attach a file to a record. Manual entry is always available.

Tasks, reminders, and equipment details

If you allow notifications, HomeVera schedules a reminder the day before a task is due and on the morning it is due. Timing is approximate. Reminders are scheduled on the phone. They are not sent from a server. You can still use the schedule if you decline notifications.

Records

Documents stay with the equipment

Save the record, link it to a piece of equipment, and attach a PDF, image, or text file up to 10 MB. The file stays on the phone. It is not uploaded.

A photo of a label can be read on the phone. The words inside a PDF are not read. If you attach a PDF, you fill in the title and notes yourself.

Service

Book service opens your own Messages or Mail

You save one provider for each piece of equipment by typing the name, phone, and email. HomeVera does not import your contacts, and it does not keep a directory of companies.

Text or Email then opens the Messages or Mail app already on your phone, with the equipment details filled in. HomeVera does not send the message and does not receive the reply. You can also add the visit to your calendar as an .ics file and mark the task scheduled.

Your records stay on the phone

  • No HomeVera account and no sign-in.
  • No ads, no analytics, and no tracking in the app.
  • Home records, photos, and files are stored on the phone. HomeVera does not run a server for them.
  • No cloud sync and no backup from HomeVera. Records are not transferred to a new owner.
  • The planned App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.
  • The planned Google Play label is No data collected or shared.

Questions

Is there an account?

No. There is no sign-in and no HomeVera account. A new install starts empty.

Where are the records stored?

On the phone, in the app's private storage. HomeVera does not provide cloud sync or backup, and it cannot restore records or move them to a new owner. Keep your own copies of important documents.

What does a photo do?

Take a photo or choose a photo. The text is read on the phone, then you review an editable screen. Each field is marked “Filled in from your photo” or “Not found in the photo”. Nothing is saved until Confirm & save. You can type the details instead. The words inside a PDF are not read.

How do reminders work?

Notifications are optional. If you allow them, maintenance reminders are scheduled on the phone for the day before a task is due and the morning it is due. Warranty alerts are scheduled 60, 30, and 7 days before the end date. Timing is approximate. Nothing is sent through a push service.

Does Book service send a message?

No. Text or Email opens your own Messages or Mail app with the equipment details filled in. HomeVera does not send or receive that message. You can also add an .ics calendar file and mark the task scheduled.

What does it cost?

HomeVera is a paid app with a free trial. A price and a trial length are not listed yet. Apple and Google handle the billing. Final pricing is coming soon.
