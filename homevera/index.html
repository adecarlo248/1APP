What you can keep in HomeVera

HomeVera starts empty. Free includes one home, up to 10 equipment items and documents combined, maintenance reminders, and manual entry. Premium adds the items marked below.

Equipment and appliances

Free

Type the model, serial number, location, purchase date, install date, and warranty dates. Dates you enter are not checked with a manufacturer. Free includes one home and up to 10 equipment items and documents combined.

Documents

Free, within the combined limit

Keep a receipt, warranty, manual, invoice, quote, insurance, permit, or service record, and link it to the equipment it belongs to. You can attach a PDF, image, or text file up to 10 MB. HomeVera does not read the words inside a PDF. Equipment items and documents share one limit of 10 on Free.

Maintenance tasks

Free

Add a task with a due date. Reschedule it, and mark it complete with notes, the service provider, and the cost. Maintenance reminders are included. The history stays with the home on that phone.

Expiring soon

Free

The home screen includes an Expiring soon list for warranties ending within 60 days. That list is included with Free.

Premium

Premium adds warranty reminder notifications 60, 30, and 7 days before the end date you entered. If you allow notifications, those reminders are scheduled on the phone. They are not sent from a server.

How it works

Manual entry is included with Free. Photo scan is part of Premium.

  1. Set up your home Add your name and details such as the address, year built, size, heating, and cooling. There is no account to create. Free is one home.
  2. Add equipment by hand Type the manufacturer, model, and serial number. That manual entry is included with Free, up to 10 equipment items and documents combined.
  3. Photo scan is Premium Take a photo or choose a photo. Text is read on the phone. Each field is marked “Filled in from your photo” or “Not found in the photo”. You can edit every field. Nothing is saved until you tap Confirm & save.
  4. Add maintenance Add tasks and reminders. Those reminders are included with Free. A service provider is typed in. HomeVera does not import contacts. Book service and calendar export are part of Premium.

Premium

Photo scan reads text from a photo

Photograph an equipment label, or choose one JPEG, PNG, or WebP photo up to 5 MB. HomeVera reads the printed text on the phone. English labels are the ones it is built to read. The photo is not uploaded.

The review screen is editable. Fields it could read are marked “Filled in from your photo”. Fields it could not read are marked “Not found in the photo”. Leave anything unknown blank. Nothing is saved until you tap Confirm & save. You can cancel instead.

The label photo is not kept after you confirm or cancel, unless you separately attach a file to a record. Manual entry is always available.

Tasks, reminders, and equipment details

Maintenance reminders and the Expiring soon list are included with Free. If you allow notifications, HomeVera schedules a maintenance reminder the day before a task is due and on the morning it is due. Timing is approximate. Reminders are scheduled on the phone. They are not sent from a server. You can still use the schedule if you decline notifications. Warranty reminder notifications, 60, 30 and 7 days before the end date, are part of Premium.

Records

Documents stay with the equipment

Save the record, link it to a piece of equipment, and attach a PDF, image, or text file up to 10 MB. The file stays on the phone. It is not uploaded.

Reading text from a photo is Premium. The words inside a PDF are not read. If you attach a PDF, you fill in the title and notes yourself.

Premium

Book service opens your own Messages or Mail

You save one provider for each piece of equipment by typing the name, phone, and email. HomeVera does not import your contacts, and it does not keep a directory of companies.

Text or Email then opens the Messages or Mail app already on your phone, with the equipment details filled in. HomeVera does not send the message and does not receive the reply. Premium also includes calendar export: an .ics file you can add on your phone, and you can mark the task scheduled.

Your records stay on the phone

  • No HomeVera account and no sign-in.
  • No ads, no analytics, and no tracking in the app.
  • Home records, photos, and files are stored on the phone. HomeVera does not run a server for them.
  • No cloud sync and no backup from HomeVera. Records are not transferred to a new owner.
  • The planned App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.
  • The planned Google Play label is No data collected or shared.

Questions

Is there an account?

No. There is no sign-in and no HomeVera account. A new install starts empty.

Where are the records stored?

On the phone, in the app's private storage. HomeVera does not provide cloud sync or backup, and it cannot restore records or move them to a new owner. Keep your own copies of important documents.

What does a photo do?

Photo scan is part of Premium. Take a photo or choose a photo. The text is read on the phone, then you review an editable screen. Each field is marked “Filled in from your photo” or “Not found in the photo”. Nothing is saved until Confirm & save. Manual entry is included with Free. The words inside a PDF are not read.

How do reminders work?

Maintenance reminders and the Expiring soon list are included with Free. Notifications are optional. If you allow them, a maintenance reminder is scheduled on the phone for the day before a task is due and the morning it is due. Warranty reminder notifications are part of Premium: 60, 30, and 7 days before the end date. Timing is approximate. Nothing is sent through a push service.

Does Book service send a message?

Book service and calendar export are part of Premium. Text or Email opens your own Messages or Mail app with the equipment details filled in. HomeVera does not send or receive that message. You can also add an .ics calendar file and mark the task scheduled.
