What you can keep in HomeVera

HomeVera starts empty. Free includes one home, up to 10 equipment items and documents combined, maintenance reminders, and manual entry. Premium adds the items marked below.

Equipment and appliances

Free

Type the model, serial number, location, purchase date, install date, and warranty dates. Dates you enter are not checked with a manufacturer. Free includes one home and up to 10 equipment items and documents combined.

Documents

Free, within the combined limit

Keep a receipt, warranty, manual, invoice, quote, insurance, permit, or service record, and link it to the equipment it belongs to. You can attach an image, a text file, or another document file up to 10 MB. HomeVera does not read the words inside an attached document file. Equipment items and documents share one limit of 10 on Free.

Maintenance tasks

Free

Add a task with a due date. Reschedule it, and mark it complete with notes, the service provider, and the cost. Maintenance reminders are included. The history stays with the home.

Expiring soon

Free

The home screen includes an Expiring soon list for warranties ending within 60 days. That list is included with Free.

Premium

Premium adds warranty reminder notifications 60, 30, and 7 days before the end date you entered, if you allow notifications.

How it works

Manual entry is included with Free. Photo scan is part of Premium.

  1. Set up your home Sign in with an emailed magic link (no password), Sign in with Apple, or Google sign-in. Add your name and details such as the address, year built, size, heating, and cooling. Free is one home.
  2. Add equipment by hand Type the manufacturer, model, and serial number. That manual entry is included with Free, up to 10 equipment items and documents combined.
  3. Photo scan is Premium Take a photo or choose a photo. Text is read on the phone. Each field is marked “Filled in from your photo” or “Not found in the photo”. You can edit every field. Nothing is saved until you tap Confirm & save.
  4. Add maintenance Add tasks and reminders. Those reminders are included with Free. A service provider is typed in. HomeVera does not import contacts. Book service and Add to calendar are part of Premium.

Premium

Photo scan reads text from a photo

Photograph an equipment label, or choose one JPEG, PNG, or WebP photo up to 5 MB. HomeVera reads the printed text on the phone. English labels are the ones it is built to read. A photo you save is part of your home data, which is stored in Canada.

The review screen is editable. Fields it could read are marked “Filled in from your photo”. Fields it could not read are marked “Not found in the photo”. Leave anything unknown blank. Nothing is saved until you tap Confirm & save. You can cancel instead.

The label photo is not kept after you confirm or cancel, unless you separately attach a file to a record. Manual entry is always available.

Tasks, reminders, and equipment details

Maintenance reminders and the Expiring soon list are included with Free. If you allow notifications, HomeVera schedules a maintenance reminder the day before a task is due and on the morning it is due. Timing is approximate. You can still use the schedule if you decline notifications. Warranty reminder notifications, 60, 30 and 7 days before the end date, are part of Premium.

Records

Documents stay with the equipment

Save the record, link it to a piece of equipment, and attach an image, a text file, or another document file up to 10 MB. Saved files are part of your home data, which is stored in Canada.

Reading text from a photo is Premium. HomeVera does not read the words inside an attached document file. You fill in the title and notes yourself.

Premium

Book service opens your own Messages or Mail

You save one provider for each piece of equipment by typing the name, phone, and email. HomeVera does not import your contacts, and it does not keep a directory of companies.

Text or Email then opens the Messages or Mail app already on your phone, with the equipment details filled in. HomeVera does not send the message and does not receive the reply. Premium also includes Add to calendar: an .ics file you can add on your phone, and you can mark the task scheduled.

Your home data is stored in Canada

  • Your home data is stored in Canada. Supabase hosts the database, sign-in, file storage, and backups in Canada (Central). Vendor billing, support access, and some platform logs may be processed outside Canada.
  • Sign in with an emailed magic link, Sign in with Apple, or Google sign-in, so your home is backed up and restored on a new phone.
  • Privacy first: HomeVera does not sell leads or data, and the app has no ad or analytics SDKs.
  • Delete your account anytime, in the app or from the deletion page.
