Privacy policy

HomeVera privacy policy

Draft – pending lawyer review. This is not the finished store policy.

Effective date: Draft – November 2026. This draft covers HomeVera for iPhone, iPad and Android.

Who we are

HomeVera is published by 1APP Technologies Inc., based in Ontario, Canada (“we” or “us”). 1APP Technologies Inc. is responsible for privacy questions about HomeVera.

Contact: support@use1app.com. You can also write to adecarlo@use1app.com. A mailing address is not included in this draft.

The short version

HomeVera stores your home records on your phone. There is no HomeVera account, and we don't run a server that receives those records. We don't receive your records, photos, or files from the app. The app has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

The planned Apple App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected. The planned Google Play data-safety label is No data collected or shared. Those labels describe the app. They are the labels planned for the store listings.

What the app stores on your phone

Your home profile: your name (optional), your home's address, and details you choose to add, such as year built, size, rooms, heating, cooling, and purchase date.

Equipment: names, manufacturers, models, serial numbers, locations, and purchase, install, and warranty dates. You can also save a service provider's name, phone, and email that you type in. HomeVera does not import contacts.

Maintenance: tasks, due dates, completion history, notes, service provider names, and costs.

Documents: record titles and notes, and any PDF, image, or text file you attach (up to 10 MB each). Document types are receipt, warranty, manual, invoice, quote, insurance, permit, and service record.

This information is kept in HomeVera's private storage on your device. We can't see it from the app, and the app does not send it to us or to anyone else.

Camera and photos

HomeVera uses the camera only when you tap Take a photo. To choose an existing photo, it opens the system photo picker and receives only the one photo you pick. It does not get access to your whole photo library.

The photo is read on your phone by text recognition built into the app. It is for English text in a JPEG, PNG, or WebP image up to 5 MB. It is not sent to us or to any other company. You get an editable review screen. A field is marked “Filled in from your photo” or “Not found in the photo”. Nothing is saved until you tap Confirm & save. The photo is not kept after you confirm or cancel, unless you separately attach it to a record.

Documents and PDFs

You can attach a PDF, image, or text file to a record. The file stays on the phone. HomeVera does not read the words inside a PDF. You fill in the record title and notes yourself.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, HomeVera schedules reminders on your phone. Maintenance reminders are for the day before a task is due and the morning it is due. Warranty alerts are 60, 30, and 7 days before the end date you entered. They are not sent through a push service or from a server. Timing is approximate. A reminder shows the task or warranty name, and depending on your phone's settings it may appear on your lock screen. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings. You can keep using the schedule if you decline them.

Book service

Book service does not send a message. Text or Email opens the Messages or Mail app on your phone, with equipment details filled in, addressed to the provider you typed in. HomeVera does not send that message and does not receive the reply. Add to calendar creates an .ics file for you to save. The provider's name, phone, and email stay in the equipment record on your phone.

Questions about your records

If you type a question about the records saved on your phone, HomeVera looks up those records on the phone. The question is not sent off the phone.

What we don't do

We don't collect, sell, rent, or share your personal information from the app. HomeVera doesn't show ads, use analytics or tracking tools, or track you across other apps and websites. It doesn't access your location, contacts, or microphone.

The app does not send your records over the internet. Android lists an internet permission because it is included by the framework the app is built on. HomeVera does not use it to send your records.

Purchases

HomeVera is a paid app with a free trial. Final pricing and the trial length are not set in this draft. Apple and Google handle the purchase and the trial. HomeVera does not receive your payment card details and does not collect purchase data from Apple or Google.

Information we receive outside the app

If you email support@use1app.com or adecarlo@use1app.com, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it to reply to you. How long those emails are kept is not set in this draft.

Apple and Google may give the publisher summary statistics, such as download counts, and crash reports if you have allowed sharing them in your device settings. Their own privacy policies govern that information. That is separate from the records in the app. It does not change the planned store labels above.

Backups, new phones, and a new owner

HomeVera does not provide cloud sync or backup. It does not transfer your records to a new phone for you, and it does not transfer them to a new owner. We don't have a copy of your records and can't restore them.

iPhone: HomeVera's records are kept in the app's private storage, which iOS can include in an iCloud or computer backup that you control.

Android: HomeVera opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so your records don't move to a new phone automatically.

Security

Your records are protected by your phone's operating system and screen lock. HomeVera does not add its own encryption. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open the records. Use a passcode or screen lock on your device.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your records stay until you delete them. You can delete individual records and files, or use “Erase records on this device” to remove HomeVera's records and attached files on that device. Uninstalling the app also removes them from the app's storage. Copies you downloaded or exported, and any backup your phone's system made, are outside that erase.

Your privacy rights

Because your records stay on your device, you can view, correct, and delete them yourself in the app.

For personal information we do hold, such as a support email, you can ask to access, correct, or delete it by writing to support@use1app.com. This draft says we will respond within 30 days. A lawyer should confirm that sentence before the policy is published as final.

If you live in Canada and aren't satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at https://www.priv.gc.ca/. If you live elsewhere, you may have similar rights under your local law.

Children

HomeVera is meant for homeowners and is not directed at children. This draft does not set a minimum age. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

This website

These HomeVera pages do not add analytics, advertising, or tracking tools, and they do not set analytics cookies. The host that serves the pages may keep ordinary request logs, such as an IP address and the page requested. Your home records are not on this website and are not part of those requests. Email links open your own mail app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the page and the effective date will be updated. If a future version of HomeVera adds accounts, cloud sync, or anything else that sends your records off your phone, this policy and the store privacy labels will be updated before that version is released. Where the law requires consent, HomeVera will ask first.
