Privacy policy

HomeVera privacy policy

Effective date: November 2026. Last updated: November 2026. This policy covers the HomeVera app for iPhone, iPad and Android, and these HomeVera web pages.

Support · support@use1app.com

Who we are

HomeVera is published by 1APP Technologies Inc., based in Ontario, Canada (“we” or “us”). 1APP Technologies Inc. is responsible for privacy questions about HomeVera.

Privacy contact: support@use1app.com. You can also write to adecarlo@use1app.com. The support page is https://use1app.com/homevera/support/.

The short version

Your home data is stored in Canada. You sign in to an account. HomeVera does not sell leads or data. The app has no ad or analytics SDKs. The purchase plugin talks only to Apple and Google.

Sign-in

You can sign in with an emailed magic link. There is no password. You can also use Sign in with Apple or Google sign-in. We receive the email address used for the magic link, and the name and email Apple or Google provides for that sign-in.

Information we collect

We collect:

  • Your email address.
  • Your name, when Apple or Google provides it for sign-in.
  • Home records you add, including the home profile, equipment, maintenance, and warranty dates.
  • Photos and documents you add. Document types are receipt, warranty, manual, invoice, quote, insurance, permit, service record, photo, and other.
  • Purchase status, so the app can tell whether Premium is active. @capgo/native-purchases talks to Apple or Google for that status. HomeVera does not receive your payment card details.

Home profile details you may add include the address, year built, size, rooms, heating, cooling, and purchase date. Equipment details include names, manufacturers, models, serial numbers, locations, and purchase, install, and warranty dates. You can type a service provider's name, phone, and email. HomeVera does not import contacts. Maintenance includes tasks, due dates, completion history, notes, service provider names, and costs.

We use this information to run your account, keep the home record, store photos and documents, back up that home data, transfer a home, make an export, show Premium status, and reply to you.

Camera and photos

HomeVera uses the camera only when you tap Take a photo. To choose an existing photo, it opens the system photo picker and receives only the one photo you pick. It does not get access to your whole photo library.

The photo is read on your phone by text recognition built into the app. It is for English text in a JPEG, PNG, or WebP image up to 5 MB. You get an editable review screen. A field is marked “Filled in from your photo” or “Not found in the photo”. Nothing is saved until you tap Confirm & save. A photo you save with a record is part of your home data.

Documents and PDFs

You can attach a PDF, image, or text file to a record, up to 10 MB each. HomeVera does not read the words inside a PDF. You fill in the record title and notes yourself. Saved files are part of your home data.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, HomeVera schedules reminders. Maintenance reminders are included with Free. They are for the day before a task is due and the morning it is due. Warranty alert notifications, 60, 30, and 7 days before the end date you entered, are part of Premium. Timing is approximate. A reminder shows the task or warranty name, and depending on your phone's settings it may appear on your lock screen. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings. You can keep using the schedule if you decline them. The Expiring soon list is included with Free and does not require a notification.

Book service

Book service does not send a message. Text or Email opens the Messages or Mail app on your phone, with equipment details filled in, addressed to the provider you typed in. HomeVera does not send that message and does not receive the reply. Add to calendar creates an .ics file for you to save. The provider's name, phone, and email stay in the equipment record.

What we don't do

HomeVera does not sell leads or personal information. The app has no ad or analytics SDKs. It does not use advertising or analytics tracking. It does not access your location, contacts, or microphone. HomeVera does not import contacts.

Purchases

HomeVera is free to start, with an optional Premium subscription. Free is one home, up to 10 equipment items and documents combined, maintenance reminders, manual entry, and the Expiring soon list. Premium is $4.99 CAD a month or $39.99 CAD a year and adds unlimited equipment items and documents, photo scan, warranty reminder notifications 60, 30 and 7 days before the end date, Book service, and calendar export. Prices are in Canadian dollars. Taxes may apply where required. Premium is not available to buy on this website. Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

Premium is an auto-renewing subscription. When the app is listed, it will be billed through Apple (the App Store) or Google Play. It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. The store account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the period ends. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in the device's App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

Premium includes a 14-day free trial. The trial converts to a paid subscription unless it is cancelled before it ends. Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited when a subscription is purchased, where that applies.

Refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their policies. 1APP Technologies Inc. does not receive payment card details. Purchase and subscription status is processed by Apple or Google. HomeVera does not keep a copy of the payment card or the purchase for its own records.

Libraries included with the app

The HomeVera build this policy describes includes these on-device libraries, and no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDK:

  • Capacitor, including its filesystem, camera, local notifications, app, and splash screen plugins. These run on the phone to store files, open the camera or photo picker, schedule local reminders, and show the launch screen.
  • Tesseract.js, bundled with the app, including its English text data. It reads the text in a label photo on the phone. The photo is not sent to the authors of that library.
  • @capgo/native-purchases. It contacts Apple or Google only to buy, restore, and check Premium status. It sends no home records.

Those libraries do not sell your home records. @capgo/native-purchases contacts Apple or Google only to buy, restore, and check Premium status. It sends no home records. Your home data is stored with Supabase.

Who we share information with

Supabase is a service provider. It hosts the database, sign-in, file storage, and backups in Canada (Central), region ca-central-1. Your home data is stored in Canada. Vendor billing, support access, and some platform logs may be processed outside Canada.

Apple and Google receive what is needed for Sign in with Apple, Google sign-in, and for the purchase plugin to buy, restore, and check Premium status. The purchase plugin talks only to Apple and Google.

We do not sell leads or personal information. There are no ad or analytics SDKs.

Support email

If you email support@use1app.com or adecarlo@use1app.com, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it to reply and to handle a request, including an account-deletion request.

Support emails are kept for 90 days.

Transfer and export

You can transfer a home for free. The transfer is complete: the buyer gets the whole home record, and the seller's access ends. Before that, the seller is offered a personal export. No Premium is needed on either side.

You can export anytime. The export is a PDF of the home plus a zip of the files and data. If the service shuts down, users get at least 90 days' notice, and export stays available during that notice.

Backups

Backups of your home data are hosted with Supabase in Canada (Central). Your home data is stored in Canada.

Keeping and deleting your information

While your account is open, your home records, photos, and documents stay until you delete them or you delete the account.

You can delete your account in the app. You can also use the delete-account page, which opens an email to support@use1app.com in your own mail app. This website does not store that request.

Home data, files, and backups are deleted within 30 days, including from backups.

You can also ask to see or correct the personal information we hold, including a support email.

Revoking consent

  • Camera: the app uses it only when you tap Take a photo. You can refuse that permission in your phone settings. Choosing a photo uses the system picker and receives only the photo you pick.
  • Notifications: you can turn them off in your phone settings.
  • Account: delete it in the app, or request deletion from the delete-account page.

We will respond to an access, correction, or deletion request within 30 days. That timing follows the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). This policy also follows the laws of Ontario, Canada, and applicable Canadian federal law.

Your privacy rights

You can ask to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold. You can delete your account in the app or from the delete-account page. You can export a PDF of the home and a zip of the files and data.

If you live in Canada and are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at https://www.priv.gc.ca/. If you live elsewhere, you may have similar rights under your local law.

Children

HomeVera is for homeowners. It is not directed to children under 13, and it is not a children's app. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

This website

These HomeVera pages do not add analytics, advertising, or tracking tools, and they do not set analytics cookies. The host that serves the pages may keep ordinary request logs, such as an IP address and the page requested. Your home records are not on this website and are not part of those requests. Email links open your own mail app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the page and the effective date will be updated. The App Store and Google Play privacy labels for the cloud version will be set to match this policy before that version is released. Where the law requires consent, HomeVera will ask first.
