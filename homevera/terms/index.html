The app

HomeVera is a homeowner app for keeping equipment and appliance records, documents, maintenance tasks, and warranty dates on your phone. It is for iPhone, iPad and Android. It is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. It is free to start, with an optional Premium subscription. Premium is not available to buy on this website.

Free includes one home, up to 10 equipment items and documents combined, maintenance reminders, and manual entry. Premium adds unlimited equipment items and documents, photo scan (reading text from a photo), warranty alerts, Book service, and calendar export. You can type equipment details yourself. Photo scan reads text on the device, and nothing is saved until you confirm. Book service opens your own Messages or Mail app, or an .ics calendar file. HomeVera does not send those messages.

No account

There is no HomeVera account and no sign-in. A new install starts empty. The records you add stay on that device.

Your records

You are responsible for the information you enter and the files you attach. HomeVera stores them in the app's private storage on the device. 1APP Technologies Inc. does not receive those records from the app and does not provide cloud sync, backup, or a transfer to a new phone or a new owner.

Keep your own copies of important documents. Deleting a record, erasing HomeVera's data, or uninstalling the app removes what is stored in the app on that device. Copies you already exported, and backups your phone's system made, are outside that delete.

On iPhone, the phone's own backup can include the app's private storage if you have that backup turned on. On Android, HomeVera opts out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.

Photos and documents

Take a photo or choose a photo, and the app reads text on the device. You must review every field before Confirm & save. The reading can miss or misread text. Manual entry is always available. The words inside a PDF are not read. Attached files are PDF, image, or text files up to 10 MB, and label photos for reading are JPEG, PNG, or WebP up to 5 MB.

Reminders

Notifications are optional and local to the phone. Maintenance reminders are scheduled for the day before a task is due and the morning it is due. Warranty alerts are scheduled 60, 30, and 7 days before the end date you entered. Timing is approximate. HomeVera does not promise that a reminder will appear at an exact minute, or that you will see it.

Book service

You type the provider. HomeVera does not import contacts and does not contact the provider. Text, Email, and the calendar file are handled by the apps on your phone. You are responsible for what you send. HomeVera does not send or receive those messages.

Dates, scores, and what HomeVera does not decide

Warranty status uses the dates you enter. HomeVera does not check those dates with a manufacturer or an insurer. The organization score starts at 100 and drops for overdue tasks and expired warranties recorded in the app. It is not a home inspection, a safety assessment, or a statement about the condition of a building or appliance.
