The app

Domoro is a homeowner app for keeping equipment and appliance records, documents, maintenance tasks, and warranty dates on your phone. It is for iPhone, iPad and Android. It is coming soon to the App Store and Google Play. It is free to start, with an optional Premium subscription. Premium is not available to buy on this website.

Free includes one home, up to 10 equipment items and documents combined, maintenance reminders, manual entry, and the Expiring soon list. Premium adds unlimited equipment items and documents, photo scan (reading text from a photo), warranty reminder notifications 60, 30 and 7 days before the end date, Book service, and Add to calendar. You can type equipment details yourself. Photo scan reads text on the device, and nothing is saved until you confirm. Book service opens your own Messages or Mail app, or an .ics calendar file. Domoro does not send those messages.

Your account

You sign in with an emailed magic link. There is no password. You can also use Sign in with Apple or Google sign-in. Your home data is stored in Canada. Supabase hosts the database, sign-in, file storage, and backups in Canada (Central). Vendor billing, support access, and some platform logs may be processed outside Canada.

Your records

You are responsible for the information you enter and the files you attach. Saved home records, photos, and documents are your home data. Sign in so your home is backed up and restored on a new phone.

You can request a copy of your data by emailing support@use1app.com; we will provide it within 30 days. If the service shuts down, you get at least 90 days' notice. During that time, you can request a copy of your data.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app. If you have uninstalled the app, the deletion page opens an email to support@use1app.com. This website does not store that request.

Home data, files, and backups are deleted within 30 days, including from backups.

Support emails are kept for 90 days.

Photos and documents

Take a photo or choose a photo, and the app reads text on the device. You must review every field before Confirm & save. The reading can miss or misread text. Manual entry is always available. Domoro does not read the words inside an attached document file. Attached files are an image, a text file, or another document file up to 10 MB, and label photos for reading are JPEG, PNG, or WebP up to 5 MB.

Reminders

Notifications are optional. Maintenance reminders are scheduled for the day before a task is due and the morning it is due. The Expiring soon list is included with Free. Warranty reminder notifications, scheduled 60, 30, and 7 days before the end date you entered, are part of Premium. Timing is approximate. Domoro does not promise that a reminder will appear at an exact minute, or that you will see it.

Book service

You type the provider. Domoro does not import contacts and does not contact the provider. Text, Email, and the calendar file are handled by the apps on your phone. You are responsible for what you send. Domoro does not send or receive those messages.

Dates, scores, and what Domoro does not decide

Warranty status uses the dates you enter. Domoro does not check those dates with a manufacturer or an insurer. The organization score starts at 100 and drops for overdue tasks and expired warranties recorded in the app. It is not a home inspection, a safety assessment, or a statement about the condition of a building or appliance.
