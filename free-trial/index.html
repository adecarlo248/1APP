Performance-Based Offer

Turn old leads into new revenue.

Run a 14-day Revenue Reactivation Sprint with no upfront setup fee. 1APP earns only when the agreed, tracked result occurs.

What the Sprint includes

  • Review and segmentation of an approved dormant lead or past-customer list
  • A 14-day AI-assisted text campaign built around one approved offer
  • Reply qualification, booking, stop rules, opt-out handling, and campaign tracking
  • No upfront setup fee; messaging usage and the written success fee are disclosed before launch
  • A results review and recommendation for any broader 1APP automation

Who qualifies

  • A usable list of eligible dormant contacts—250 minimum; 1,000+ is ideal
  • A clear offer and capacity to answer, quote, and close resulting opportunities
  • Written confirmation that the contacts may lawfully be messaged
  • Agreement to track kept appointments, signed work, collected payments, cancellations, and refunds
  • Regulated industries require a separate compliance review before launch

How fees work: the fee is agreed in writing before launch, varies by niche and average completed-job value, and includes a hard cap per attributed sale. Percentage fees are based on collected revenue or disclosed gross profit—not quotes, verbal commitments, or uncollected contract value. If your business does not have a suitable dormant list, ask about a paid starter automation instead.
