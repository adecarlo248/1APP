About 1APP

AI automation systems for small businesses that need faster follow-up.

1APP is built for tradespeople and local service businesses. The focus is practical automation: missed-call recovery, booking calendars, reminders, review requests, nurture workflows, and voice reception.

The approach is simple: make it easier for a good business to capture the opportunities it already has. No bloated tech stack. No buzzword-heavy setup. Just a clean system that answers faster, follows up consistently, and keeps the owner focused on the work.

Built for Ontario trades

Designed around local service businesses that need reliable lead capture without learning a new platform.

Workflow-first approach

Built around booking tools and simple follow-up systems that fit how your business already runs.

Managed setup, no headaches

Clear tiers, supported onboarding, and no tech headaches. We handle the build so you do not have to.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Most setups are live within 3 to 5 business days after the demo. We map your lead flow, build the workflows, connect your calendar and phone, test everything, and hand you a working system.

No. 1APP is a managed service. We build, connect, and maintain the automations for you. You interact with the system the same way your customers do: through calls, texts, and your booking calendar.

The booking lands directly on your calendar. The automation sends a confirmation text immediately, followed by a 24-hour reminder and a 1-hour reminder. Once the lead books, the follow-up sequence stops so you do not get duplicate messages.

The AI voice receptionist answers calls naturally, asks qualifying questions based on your business info, collects the caller's name and callback number, and books the appointment into your calendar. Callers are handled professionally whether or not you can pick up.

Yes. Most businesses start with Standard to turn on the core communication, reputation, payment, and launch tools, then upgrade to Growth or Revenue Engine as they see results. Setup fees for upgrades are minimal since the core system is already in place.

Every business has a different phone setup, calendar system, and workflow. After a free demo we quote a one-time setup fee based on your specific needs. There are no hidden recurring charges beyond your monthly plan.
