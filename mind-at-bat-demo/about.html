Founder's Message

Baseball has always been more than a game.

Get in Touch

Baseball has always been more than a game to me.

Like many people, I've faced my own mental health challenges, and throughout those difficult times, baseball gave me something to hold onto. It provided structure, purpose, community, and an outlet when I needed it most. The lessons I learned on the field helped me navigate battles that couldn't always be seen by others.

That's why Mind At Bat was created.

This brand is built around my love for baseball and my belief that mental health matters. Every design, every collection, and every story we share reflects the connection between the game we love and the challenges we face beyond the diamond.

For now, our focus remains on baseball. It's the community we know, the community we love, and a community that Justin and I have been fortunate to be a part of through the relationships we've built over the years. While Mind At Bat may grow and evolve in the future, baseball will always be at the heart of where we started.

My hope is that this brand sparks conversations, creates connections, and reminds people that they are never alone in their struggles.

Because no matter who you are, we're all playing the same game.
We're just facing different battles.
