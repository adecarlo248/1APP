Founder's Message

Baseball has always been more than a game to me.

Like many people, I've faced my own mental health challenges, and throughout those difficult times, baseball gave me something to hold onto. It provided structure, purpose, community, and an outlet when I needed it most.

The lessons I learned on the field helped me navigate battles that couldn't always be seen by others. That's why Mind At Bat was created.

My hope is that this brand sparks conversations, creates connections, and reminds people that they are never alone in their struggles.

Because no matter who you are, we're all playing the same game. We're just facing different battles.

— Carter ClarkeFounder, Mind At Bat
