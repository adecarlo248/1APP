Secure contact-list upload

The client uploads their authorized business contacts through a private 1APP page. Do not ask them to email, text, or WhatsApp the file.

Rod: simple explanation to read to the client
“We need only the past leads and customers your business is allowed to contact—not your whole personal phone book. I’ll send you a secure 1APP upload link. If your contacts are on Android or Google, export the selected contacts as a CSV. If they are on an iPhone, export them from iCloud Contacts as a vCard. Open the link, choose the file, check the two confirmation boxes, and press Upload. If you get stuck, stop there and I’ll walk you through it.”

Android/Google: Go to contacts.google.com → create a label named “1APP Reactivation” → add eligible contacts → Export → Google CSV.

iPhone: Go to icloud.com/contacts on a computer → select eligible contacts → Export vCard → upload the .vcf file.

Rod must repeat: remove personal contacts, employees, active opportunities, recent estimates, and anyone who opted out.
