Questions

Straight answers.

Do my employees need to pay or enter a card?

No. The company subscription covers everyone. Employees join with your crew code and their own PIN and count toward your plan's user limit.

Does CrewClock track my crew's location all day?

No. Location is only recorded at the moment someone clocks in or out (if they allow location permission), or when a manager deliberately captures a job-site location. There's no continuous or background tracking.

Does the owner count as a user?

Yes. The 5, 10 and 20 user limits include every active user, including the owner.

How does the free trial work?

Checkout currently includes a 14-day trial with a card required. After checkout, return to CrewClock in the same browser to create your company — we verify your subscription with Stripe before opening access. Please don't pay twice; if anything looks off, contact support.

Can I cancel or change plans?

Yes. Owners can open Manage billing from the CrewClock web app to update or cancel the existing subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing or trial period.

Does CrewClock replace my payroll software?

No. CrewClock organizes accurate hours, overtime and gross-pay estimates and sends timesheets to your payroll person. Your payroll provider or bookkeeper still handles deductions, remittances and paying your team.

What phones does it work on?

CrewClock runs in any modern phone browser today, so iPhone and Android crews can both use it right away.
