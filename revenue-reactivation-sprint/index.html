What the Sprint includes

  • Review and segmentation of client-authorized past leads and customers
  • A dedicated local number, connected calendar, and 14-day AI-assisted messaging window
  • Client-branded messages, reply qualification, booking, human takeover, stop rules, opt-out handling, and campaign tracking
  • No upfront setup fee; messaging usage, the fixed fee per verified estimate, and the campaign ceiling are agreed before launch
  • A results review and recommendation for any broader 1APP automation

Who qualifies

  • A usable list of at least 50 client-authorized past leads or customers
  • A clear offer and capacity to answer, quote, and close resulting opportunities
  • Written confirmation that the contacts may lawfully be messaged
  • Agreement to document kept qualified appointments and written estimates during weekly reconciliation
  • Regulated industries require a separate compliance review before launch

What counts as a verified estimate: the contact keeps the agreed qualified appointment, meets the written qualification rules, and the business issues an evidenced written estimate—normally within 48 hours. A booking, no-show, cancellation, duplicate, active pre-Sprint opportunity, verbal price, or undocumented estimate is not billable. The fixed fee and maximum campaign total are agreed in writing before launch. The 14-day clock starts only after the list, dedicated number, calendar, messages, compliance attestation, and testing are ready.
