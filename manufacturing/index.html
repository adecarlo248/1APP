The chasing

A request comes in. It needs an owner, a reply to the buyer, and a follow-up when it goes quiet. The estimator, rep, or tech still decides the work.

How a request moves

  1. 1
    Step 1 RFQ / service-request intake A quote request or service request is captured as it arrives.
  2. 2
    Step 2 Instant buyer acknowledgement by text and email The buyer gets a text and an email that the request was received.
  3. 3
    Step 3 Routing to the right estimator, rep or tech The request goes to the estimator, rep, or tech who should handle it.
  4. 4
    Step 4 Chasing missing specs Missing details are asked for from the buyer.
  5. 5
    Step 5 Follow-up on quotes that go quiet A quote that has gone quiet gets a follow-up.
  6. 6
    Step 6 Order or job status texts The buyer gets a text when the order or job status changes.
