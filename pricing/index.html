Pricing questions

Know what happens before you say yes.

What is included in the one-time setup fee?

The standard setup includes discovery validation, configuration, approved customer-facing copy, the agreed connections, testing, basic training, and launch. Anything outside the written scope is quoted before it is added.

What does the monthly fee cover?

The monthly fee covers platform access, monitoring, standard support, reporting where included, and ongoing operation of the agreed workflows. The exact support and change allowance appears in the written quote.

Why are phone, SMS, email, and AI charges separate?

Those costs change with real usage. Keeping them separate prevents low-volume businesses from subsidizing high-volume accounts and makes the recurring service fee easier to understand.

Can a bundle be changed?

Yes. Bundles are recommended starting solutions, not locked plans. Removing or adding material scope changes the final setup and monthly price, and the revised amount is shown before acceptance.

Is the Revenue Reactivation Sprint really $0 to set up?

Yes, for an eligible Sprint there is no upfront setup fee. Messaging usage, the fixed fee per verified estimate, the verified-estimate definition, and the campaign ceiling are all agreed in writing before launch. A booking or no-show alone is not billable.

Are the published delivery times guaranteed?

They are typical estimates in business days. The clock starts after the quote, scope, handoff, access, and technical acceptance are complete. Client delays, third-party approvals, porting, compliance review, unavailable APIs, failed client testing, or scope changes can pause or extend it.

Are taxes included?

No. All published prices are Canadian dollars before HST. Applicable tax is shown separately on the quote and invoice.

What happens on the pricing call?

We identify the bottleneck, confirm whether the standard scope fits, explain usage costs, validate the delivery dependencies, and recommend the smallest system that solves the problem. You receive a written quote before work begins.
