What Is Automated Lead Follow-Up?

Automated lead follow-up is a sequence of pre-written messages — texts, emails, or both — that your system sends to a new lead automatically, on a timed schedule, from the moment they first contact you until they book, buy, or opt out.

There's no one sending these manually. No VA checking a spreadsheet. No owner trying to remember who called last Tuesday. The system handles it entirely. When a lead comes in — whether from a missed call, a website form, a Facebook ad, or a referral who sent them your number — the sequence kicks off immediately and keeps running in the background while you do your actual job.

The messages are personalized (they include the lead's name and can reference how they found you), sent at strategic intervals, and stop automatically when the person replies or books an appointment. Nobody gets spammed. Leads that are ready get moved to booking. Leads that aren't ready yet get nurtured until they are.

The core insight: Most leads don't buy the first time they hear from you. They buy after the 5th or 6th touchpoint — but almost no small business owner has the discipline to follow up 5 times manually. Automation does it without forgetting, without getting tired, and without feeling awkward about it.

What a High-Converting Automated Follow-Up Sequence Looks Like

Not all follow-up sequences are equal. A good one is built around how real buyers make decisions — fast at the start (when urgency is highest), then tapering off as the lead moves through their own decision process.

Here's the structure 1APP uses for trades businesses:

Touchpoint Timing Channel Goal
1 — Instant Response Within 2 minutes of inquiry SMS Acknowledge immediately, keep them from calling a competitor
2 — Booking Nudge 1 hour later (if no reply) SMS Offer easy next step — a booking link or reply prompt
3 — Value Follow-Up Day 1 evening Email Share what makes your business different, include social proof
4 — Check-In Day 3 SMS Casual, low-pressure check-in — "still looking for help with X?"
5 — Social Proof Day 5 Email Share a customer review or testimonial relevant to their situation
6 — Last Chance Day 10 SMS Create mild urgency — let them know you're keeping a spot for them
7 — Long-Term Nurture Day 21 onward Email SMS Monthly touchpoint for leads that weren't ready — keeps you top of mind

The sequence stops the moment a lead books an appointment. They don't keep getting messages after they've already converted — that would be annoying. The system is smart enough to remove them from the follow-up queue and move them into your booking confirmation and reminder flow instead.

Frequently Asked Questions About Automated Lead Follow-Up

What is automated lead follow-up?

Automated lead follow-up is a system that sends pre-written messages (texts, emails, or both) to prospects on a timed schedule — without anyone on your team doing it manually. When a new lead comes in, the automation triggers immediately and continues following up over hours, days, or weeks until the lead responds, books, or opts out.

How many times should you follow up with a lead?

Research shows that 80% of sales require at least 5 follow-up attempts, yet most small business owners give up after 1 or 2. A strong automated follow-up sequence sends 5–8 touchpoints spread over 2–3 weeks, starting within 5 minutes of the first contact and tapering to weekly check-ins after the first few days.

What's the best way to follow up with leads for a trades business?

Text message is the highest-performing channel for trades businesses — open rates exceed 95% and most messages are read within 3 minutes. The best automated follow-up sequences lead with SMS, support with email, and trigger based on behaviour (replied, booked, no response) rather than just time alone.

How fast should you follow up with a new lead?

Speed is the single biggest factor in lead conversion. Studies consistently show that businesses that respond within 5 minutes are 21x more likely to qualify the lead than those that wait 30 minutes. For contractors, where customers are usually comparing multiple quotes simultaneously, an automated instant response is a major competitive advantage.
