What Is Missed Call Text Back?

Missed call text back is a simple but powerful automation. When someone calls your business phone and you don't pick up — whether you're on a job, in a meeting, or it's after hours — the system automatically sends a text message to their number within seconds.

The message is friendly, personal, and keeps the conversation alive:

"Hey, we just missed your call! We're out on a job right now but we'll be with you shortly. What can we help you with today? Reply here and we'll get back to you ASAP."

Instead of hitting voicemail and calling the next number, most customers reply to the text — and stay in your pipeline. You get notified, review their message when you're free, and respond or book them without playing phone tag.

That's it. Simple. Effective. And it runs completely automatically with zero effort on your part.

How It Works (Step by Step)

  1. Customer calls your business number. You're on a job and can't answer. The call goes to voicemail or rings out.
  2. The automation triggers instantly. Within seconds, a text message is sent to the caller's phone number — no human involvement required.
  3. The customer gets a text, sees that your business is responsive, and replies with their need or question instead of calling a competitor.
  4. You get a notification that a new lead has come in via missed call. You see the conversation in your inbox and can reply when you're free.
  5. The lead is captured. Even if you don't respond for an hour, the customer is already in a text conversation with your business rather than someone else's.

The most important part: this works 24/7. Weekend calls at 10pm? After-hours emergencies? The system texts back every single time — and most customers are genuinely impressed by how fast your business responds.

What is missed call text back?

How does missed call text back work?

When a call comes in and goes unanswered (or goes to voicemail), the automation triggers instantly and sends a pre-written text message to the caller's number.
