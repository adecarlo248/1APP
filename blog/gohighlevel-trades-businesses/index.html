What Is GoHighLevel?

GoHighLevel (GHL) is an all-in-one sales and marketing platform originally built for marketing agencies. It launched in 2018 and has since grown to over 60,000 agency users worldwide. The platform consolidates tools that most small businesses pay for separately — a CRM, email marketing, SMS automation, landing pages, booking calendars, review management, and more — into one subscription.

For trades businesses, the most relevant GoHighLevel features are:

📞
Missed Call Text Back
Automatically texts anyone who calls and doesn't get through, keeping the lead alive while you're on the job.
🗂️
CRM & Pipelines
Visual deal pipeline to track every lead from first contact to booked job and closed invoice.
📅
Booking Calendars
Let customers self-book appointments online — with automated confirmation and reminder sequences.
💬
Automated SMS Follow-Up
Pre-built sequences that follow up with leads automatically at 1 day, 3 days, 1 week — without you lifting a finger.
⭐
Review Requests
Automatically ask satisfied customers for Google reviews after a job is complete — while the experience is still fresh.
🤖
AI Voice & Chat
AI receptionist that answers calls, qualifies leads, and books appointments when you can't get to the phone.

On paper, this is everything a trades business needs. The question is whether the platform works the way trades owners actually need it to work — and whether you can set it all up without a dedicated marketing team.

GoHighLevel Pricing for Trades Businesses

GoHighLevel's pricing looks straightforward until you dig into the usage charges. Here's the real cost breakdown:

Plan Base Price What's Included
Starter $97/month 3 sub-accounts, all core features, 24/7 support
Unlimited $297/month Unlimited sub-accounts, basic API access, phone rebilling
Agency Pro $497/month SaaS mode, advanced API, user reporting — for agencies reselling GHL

But here's what the base price doesn't tell you:

  • SMS messages: Usage-based charges apply for every text sent
  • Phone calls: Inbound and outbound calling billed per minute
  • AI features: Conversation AI, Voice AI, and Reviews AI all carry additional usage fees
  • Email sends: High volumes incur extra charges beyond the base allocation
  • Setup costs: Unless you're technically inclined, plan to pay a GHL expert $500–$2,000+ to configure your account

For a trades business running missed call text back, automated follow-up sequences, and an AI receptionist, the realistic all-in monthly cost is often $200–$400/month once usage is factored in — before setup.

The Real Problem with GoHighLevel for Trades Businesses

GoHighLevel is built for agencies, not contractors. That distinction matters more than it sounds.

Agencies have dedicated staff to configure workflows, build automations, write follow-up copy, test sequences, and troubleshoot when something breaks. When you sign up for GoHighLevel as a plumber or HVAC company, you're handed a blank canvas and expected to build the Sistine Chapel with it — while also running your business.

What "Setting It Up Yourself" Actually Looks Like

Here's a realistic picture of what it takes to get GoHighLevel properly configured for a trades business:

  • Create and configure your CRM pipeline stages (New Lead → Contacted → Quote Sent → Booked → Job Complete → Review Requested)
  • Set up your missed call text back trigger and write the message
  • Build your booking calendar with availability, buffer times, and service types
  • Write 4–6 follow-up SMS messages that don't sound robotic
  • Configure the timing for each follow-up (1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks)
  • Set up appointment confirmation and reminder automations
  • Connect your business phone number and test the entire sequence
  • Write and configure your review request message
  • Train yourself (and any staff) on how to use the conversation inbox

That's easily 20–40 hours of work before you see your first automated lead come through. And when something breaks — and things do break — you're troubleshooting inside a complex platform you're still learning.

The honest truth: GoHighLevel is an incredibly powerful platform. But raw power without the right configuration is just noise. A top-of-the-line excavator sitting in your yard doesn't grade your driveway. Trades businesses need the result, not the tool.
