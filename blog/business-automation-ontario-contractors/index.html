The 5 Business Automation Tools Every Ontario Contractor Needs

Not all automation tools are created equal, and not every contractor needs every piece of software. Here's a breakdown of the five categories where automation delivers the highest ROI for trades businesses in Ontario.

1. Missed Call Text Back

This is the single highest-ROI automation for any trades business. When a call goes unanswered — because you're on a job, in traffic, or it's 9pm — the system automatically sends a text to the caller within seconds. Instead of hitting voicemail and calling the next plumber in Google Maps, your prospect gets an instant reply and stays in your pipeline.

A plumbing business in Peterborough missing 5 calls per week, recovering even 2 of them at an average job value of $850, is looking at $6,800/month in recovered revenue. For HVAC companies in peak summer months, that number is even higher.

Read more: How Missed Call Text Back Works for Trades Businesses

2. Automated Lead Follow-Up Sequences

Most contractors follow up once. The data says it takes 5–12 touchpoints to convert a lead into a booked job. Automated follow-up sequences handle those touchpoints via text and email — spacing them out over days and weeks, keeping your name in front of the prospect without you lifting a finger.

This is especially valuable for larger jobs like roofing, HVAC replacements, or electrical panel upgrades where customers take longer to make decisions. Your automated follow-up keeps you top of mind through the whole decision cycle.

3. Online Appointment Booking

Customers expect to book online. A 24/7 booking link on your website and Google Business Profile means leads can schedule estimates and service calls at 11pm on a Sunday — without waiting for you to call back Monday morning. Automated confirmation texts and reminders then cut no-shows by up to 80%.

4. Automated Google Review Requests

Reviews drive local SEO and trust. But most contractors forget to ask — or it feels awkward. Automating the ask (a text message sent 2–4 hours after a job is marked complete) gets you 3–5x more reviews than asking manually. For Ontario contractors competing in Google Maps, more reviews means more visibility and more inbound leads.

5. Centralized Inbox & CRM

Texts, emails, Google messages, Facebook DMs — leads come from everywhere. A centralized inbox brings every conversation into one place so nothing gets missed. Paired with a basic CRM to track where each prospect is in your pipeline, this eliminates the "I forgot to call them back" problem permanently.

Getting Started: The Right Order to Implement Business Automation

The biggest mistake Ontario contractors make is trying to automate everything at once. Start with the highest-impact, lowest-complexity wins and build from there.

  1. Missed call text back first. This is live money. Every missed call you're currently losing is a lead that goes to a competitor. This automation pays for itself in the first week for most trades businesses.
  2. Appointment reminders second. If you're already booking jobs, automated reminders cut no-shows by up to 80%. The ROI is immediate — fewer empty time slots on your calendar.
  3. Post-job review requests third. Once the first two are running smoothly, add automated Google review requests. Set it up once, collect reviews forever. Your Google Maps ranking improves over months as the volume builds.
  4. Lead follow-up sequences fourth. This is where you squeeze more out of existing lead volume — nurturing the leads who said "I'll think about it" instead of letting them go cold.
  5. Online booking and centralized inbox last. These are great tools, but they have a bigger setup investment. Tackle them after the quick wins are running and generating ROI.
